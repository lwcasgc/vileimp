The game

Gearbreak is an isometric pixel-art beat 'em up roguelite. Launch robots into the air, juggle them, smash them into walls and into each other, and build a new fighting style every run.

Everyone in the city talked on the Circuit. It was noisy. It worked. Then one voice got louder than the rest: the Foundry, which promised to fix what was broken. Soon people stopped coming home, and the Foundry started building machines that never say no.

Past the last street, at a small outpost, a few friends still say no. You're their fighter.

Every run crosses nine stages: fights, survival arenas, supply depots and two bosses, the Spider-Tank and the Scrap-Golem. Combat rewards creativity. Launch robots and keep the juggle going, splat them against walls, throw them into each other, spin them with the Giant Swing or finish with a Piledriver. The more varied your moves, the higher your style multiplier climbs.

Between fights, pick parts that change how you play: ricochets, shockwaves, magnets and more. Back at the outpost, the crew helps you grow stronger for the next attempt.
