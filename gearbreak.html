The game

Gearbreak is an isometric pixel-art beat 'em up roguelite. Launch robots, juggle them, smash them into walls and each other, and build a new fighting style every run.

Everyone in the city talked on the Circuit, until the Foundry got loudest and started building machines that never say no.

Past the last street, at a small outpost, a few friends still say no. You're Rumble, their fighter.

Between fights, pick parts that change how you play, then return to the outpost stronger for the next run.

The crew

A few friends past the last street still say no to the Foundry. Here's who keeps the fight going.

  1. Rumble

    Fighter - That's you

    Motors warm. Fists ready.

    The outpost's fighter, and the one who goes back into the Foundry every run. Launch robots, juggle them, smash them into walls, then pick parts that turn each run into a new fighting style.

  2. Token

    Merchant-bot - Chassis upgrades

    I've calculated your chances of victory. I refuse to publish the figures until you win.

    Runs the outpost's most profitable shop and will tell you so. Spend your cores on permanent chassis upgrades, and expect a speech about fiscal responsibility with every sale.

  3. Rust

    Elder-bot - Moves

    I remember my first victory like it was yesterday! Can't remember yesterday, mind you.

    The oldest bot at the outpost, a veteran of wars nobody can quite date. Teaches new moves to expand your fighting style.

  4. Taps

    Barkeep-bot - Starter chips

    Loaded dice? I call them careful dice.

    Pours fresh oil, deals cards and runs the friendliest bets in town. Choose the starter chips you carry into a run, and try your luck at the table between fights.

  5. Wrench

    Engineer-bot - Utilities

    I invented a battery that lasts forever! Unfortunately, it only powers itself.

    Calls explosions "expected results". Unlock new utilities in the armory so your fighter has a trick for every situation.

Features

  1. Brawler combat built on launches, juggles, wall splats and chain reactions.
  2. Grabs and signature moves like the Piledriver, Giant Swing and Body Slam.
  3. A style multiplier that rewards mixing moves over repeating them.
  4. Runs full of fights, survival arenas, shops and bosses.
  5. Plenty of parts to build a different fighter every run.
  6. An outpost hub with a lively crew and permanent upgrades.
  7. Hand-made isometric pixel art.
